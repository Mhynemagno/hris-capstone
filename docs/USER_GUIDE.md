# User guide

- Applicants register, browse published jobs, maintain their application profile, and submit/track only their own applications.
- Employees view official records but request profile changes instead of editing official fields directly. They submit leave requests, view attendance/deployments/promotion readiness, and read notifications.
- HR manages personnel, recruitment, leave, deployments, promotions, attendance imports, and operational reports. For an active Employee account without a personnel record, HR opens **Employees**, selects **Create record**, confirms the prefilled identity, and completes the official employment fields.
- Management uses dashboards and reports only. It has no operational create, edit, approval, import, or delete action.

When an error appears, retain the form data, retry once after confirming your connection, and report the route, action, time, and visible error message to the administrator.
